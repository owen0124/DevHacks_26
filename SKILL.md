name : class-to-map

description: Say I lose track of time, and I need to get to class or office hours at a specific time. I want to set a reminder for Cubbie to notify me of a specified time in advance, how far I am away, and to automatically pull up the map to the building when a notification says, "Go now?"  The notification button has "Confirm" or "Deny." If I press Confirm, it will take me to the class using my phone's preferred map app. If the class or office hours are via Zoom, you will log me in to Zoom and have me join the waiting room. If I click deny, you will ask me if I want a reminder in 5, 15, or 30 minutes, or Decline for today.
